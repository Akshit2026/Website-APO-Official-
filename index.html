About

The AICS Parliamentary Open 2026 is a full‑day British Parliamentary debate tournament hosted at the Amsterdam International Community School (AICS) on Saturday, 14 November 2026 . The event brings together 32 teams from international, bilingual, and gymnasium schools across the Netherlands for a day of competitive debating, workshops, and adjudication led by experienced judges from National and University debating societies Across the Netherlands.

Event Details

  • Date: Saturday The 14th Of November

  • Time: 10:00-18:00

  • Location: Bijlmerplein 1000, 1102 MK Amsterdam (South East)

  • Format: British Parliamentary

Our Mission

Our mission is to give middle school and high school students across the Netherlands a platform to access high-quality, professionally adjudicated competitive debating more often. We strongly believe every young debater deserves the opportunity to compete more often, and improve their public speaking skills.

Our Vision

We at the AICS Debate Club strongly belive that British Parliamentary debating equips students with the public speaking skills that they will need for their life. Our vision is to grow English-Language debating across the Netherlands, building a stronger, more connected community of young debaters nationwide.

Partners & Sponsors

The Nederlandse Debatbond (NDB) is the official Dutch National Debate Association. Serving as a national umbrella organization, it represents roughly 1,000 members across the Netherlands, most of whom belong to university debating societies. The NDB supports the community by organizing national championships, managing rankings, and fostering competitive debate nationwide.

The Oracy Institute is an educational organization that trains students and teachers in public speaking, competitive debate, and critical thinking across both English and Dutch. Led by championship-winning competitive debaters, the institute helps youth develop Public Speaking & Crticial Thinking Skills through school curricula, teacher training programs, and specialized debate clubs.

The Erasmus Debating Society (EDS), founded in 1989, is the oldest, largest, and most prestigious university debating society in the Netherlands. Based in Rotterdam, this highly international club helps students master public speaking through weekly debates. EDS balances its competitive championship success with a vibrant social calendar of weekly drinks and events.

ASDV Bonaparte is Amsterdam’s premier student debating society, where Dutch and international students meet every Tuesday to practice public speaking and critical thinking. The club offers a highly welcoming community with no hazing rituals, balancing debating tournaments with a vibrant social calendar of weekly drinks, trips, and galas.

Registration

Register

To register for the competition, each debate team (consisting of two members) must fill in the team registration form. This year, we are introducing a registration fee of 8 euros per team, to help contribute towards competition expenses, paid by invoice on November 14th once placements are confirmed. Please note that ISA has a maximum capacity of 32 teams, and that signing up as soon as possible is recommended. We suggest that registration forms be submitted with the guide of your debate club supervisor (ideally a teacher), to ensure all details provided are accurate.

The deadline for signing up is November 2, 2026

Once you have signed up, we will contact you regarding the tournament participation fee,
